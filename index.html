Задание 7. 💼 You're the boss! — Let's start a business!

Situation: You and your partner have decided to set up a new company. You have some money to invest, but you need to agree on what kind of business to start and who you need in your team.

Your task is to create a company together and prepare to hire your first employees.

Speaking cards: Нажимай на карточку, чтобы увидеть дополнительную подсказку или полезные фразы. Обсудите все вопросы с партнёром.

Card 1 · Business idea What kind of business would you like to set up? Why? Нажми, чтобы перевернуть
Useful language I'd like to set up… What about setting up…?
I think we should…
Card 2 · Products & services What products or services will your company offer? Нажми, чтобы перевернуть
Think about What will you sell or provide? We will offer…
Our main product/service will be…
Card 3 · Customers Who will your customers be? Нажми, чтобы перевернуть
Think about Who needs your product? Our target customers are…
We want to attract…
Card 4 · What makes you special? What will make your company different from other businesses? Нажми, чтобы перевернуть
Useful language In my opinion… Our company will stand out because…
Unlike other companies,…
Card 5 · Your team How many employees will you need at the beginning? Нажми, чтобы перевернуть
Useful language We will need… employees. We should hire…
We need a full-time/part-time…
Card 6 · Make a decision Agree on your business idea and explain why it is the best choice. Нажми, чтобы перевернуть
Useful language That's a good idea, but… I agree. / I don't quite agree.
Let's close the deal!
Useful language:
I'd like to set up… · I think we should… · What about setting up…? · That's a good idea, but… · I agree / I don't quite agree. · In my opinion…
